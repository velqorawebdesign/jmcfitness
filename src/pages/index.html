Questions

Do I need a gym?

No. Tell me what equipment you have and the training plan is written around it.

Is the nutrition plan a strict diet?

No. It is built around foods you already like, with targets and simple swaps rather than a list of banned foods. It is not a medical or clinical diet. If you have a medical condition or a history of disordered eating, please speak to your GP or a registered dietitian first.

How much does it cost?

Price depends on which plan you choose. You will get the full price in euro, in writing, before you pay anything.

Can I cancel?

Yes. You have 14 days to cancel. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for how this works once your plan has been started or delivered.

Who can sign up?

Plans are for adults aged 18 and over.
