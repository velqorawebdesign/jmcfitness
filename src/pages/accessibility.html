Accessibility Statement

Last updated: {{UPDATED}}

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • All text meets WCAG AA colour contrast against its background.
  • Every image has a text alternative, or is marked as decorative.
  • You can use the whole site with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Form fields have visible labels, required fields are marked, and errors are described in text and announced to screen readers.
  • Buttons and links describe what they do, and links that open a new tab say so.
  • Moving text can be paused with the “Pause animation” button, and all animation is switched off if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • The site works when zoomed to 400% and on small screens.

Known limitations

  • The enquiry form opens your email app. If you don’t have one set up, email {{EMAIL}} directly or message us on Instagram.
  • Content we post on Instagram is outside this website and may not meet the same standard.

Feedback

If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in a different format, email {{EMAIL}} and we will help.
