Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains your right to cancel and how refunds work for JMC Fitness Plans. It forms part of our Terms & Conditions and does not affect your statutory rights.

Your 14-day right to cancel

Because you buy from us at a distance (online, by email or message), you have the right to cancel your contract within 14 days of the day the contract is formed, without giving a reason.

If we have already started

Custom Plans are written personally for you. We will only start work on your Plan within the 14-day period if you expressly ask us to, in writing. If you do:

  • If you cancel after we have started but before your Plan is delivered, we will refund the price minus a fair amount for the work already done, in proportion to the full Plan.
  • If we have fully delivered your Plan, and you asked us to start within the 14 days and confirmed in writing that you understood you would lose the right to cancel once it was fully delivered, you can no longer cancel.

We will ask for this request and acknowledgement in writing before starting. If you do not give it, we will wait until the 14 days have passed before starting your Plan.

How to cancel

Tell us clearly that you want to cancel, by email to [your-email@example.com] or by letter to [Business postal address, Ireland]. You can use the model form below, but you don’t have to. It is enough to send your message before the 14 days end.

If something is wrong with your Plan

Separately from the 14-day right, under the Consumer Rights Act 2022 your Plan must be provided with reasonable care and skill and match what we agreed. If it does not, tell us and we will put it right (for example by correcting or rewriting the Plan) at no cost to you. If we cannot do this within a reasonable time or without significant inconvenience to you, you are entitled to a price reduction or a refund, depending on the problem.

Refunds

  • We refund within 14 days of receiving your cancellation (or of agreeing a refund for a problem).
  • We refund using the same payment method you used, unless you agree otherwise. You will not be charged a fee for the refund.
